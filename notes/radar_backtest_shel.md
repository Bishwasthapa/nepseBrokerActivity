## 1. The Distribution Phase (Late August)
From **Aug 31 to Sept 3**, the price was hovering steadily around 290-294. Most retail traders looking at the price chart would interpret this as healthy consolidation. 

However, our engine repeatedly flagged the stock as **`EARLY_EXHAUSTION`**. 

**Why?** Look at the ratios on Sept 3:
* The `S-Ratio` (22-day standard trend) was sitting at 0.60.
* The `F-Ratio` (3-day fast window) had completely died to **0.55**.

The volume was drying up faster than the lagging baseline. The engine successfully identified that institutional momentum was dead, warning you to stay away or exit before a potential breakdown.

## 2. The Accumulation Phase (Mid September)
Suddenly, on **Sept 11**, the price ticked up slightly to 300. The engine instantly tripped the **`PRE_BREAKOUT_WATCH`** alarm.

**Why?**
A standard momentum scanner (5v22) would ignore this stock because the `S-Ratio` was only at **0.83** (meaning recent turnover is still *below* the 22-day average). 

But our Fast Window (3v10) caught the stealth entry: The `F-Ratio` exploded to **1.30**. Institutional money aggressively entered over that 3-day window, heavily outpacing the long-term trend (1.30 > 0.83).

By **Sept 15**, the stock ripped to 308. Our engine had you on watch 4 days earlier at 300, catching the exact inflection point of liquidity expansion.
